About the Conference

The Cyprus Academy of Sciences, Letters and Arts in collaboration with the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence co-organize the "Cyprus AI Conference", on 26-27 October 2026, at the University of Cyprus.

The conference aims to discuss the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence across sectors. From employment and ethics to the economy, government, critical infrastructure and smart cities, cybersecurity and resilience, this interdisciplinary conference highlights both the opportunities and challenges of AI in the rapidly evolving environment of Cyprus.

The Conference will be conducted in English.
